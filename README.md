# DexStash Issues

This repo is the public issue tracker for **DexStash**, a Pokémon card collection app for
iOS and Android. Scan a card, and it's identified and filed into your collection, organised
by era and set, with chase cards priced and ranked.

The app's source lives in a private repository, so bugs and feature requests are tracked
here instead.

## Reporting a bug

Please include:
- What you were doing (e.g. "scanning a card from Scarlet & Violet")
- What you expected to happen
- What happened instead
- Your device and OS version

## Requesting a feature

Open an issue describing what you'd like to see and why.
